## 🌐 About Expel

**Expel, LLP** is an independent technology company founded by **Asher** and **Slavic**. We are dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what is possible on modern digital platforms, building robust software designed for high-scale online communities, brands, and ecosystems.

---

## ⚡ How Expel Works

### 1. Independent & Self-Directed
We design, engineer, and operate our own proprietary products. We take **no client work, no outside venture money, and no roadmap by committee**. Every system we create is guided purely by engineering excellence and direct product conviction.

### 2. High-Throughput Scale
Software built by Expel reaches over **17.1 million users** across communities worldwide, engineered to a strict **99.99% uptime target** run by a dedicated, focused team.

### 3. Purpose-Built Infrastructure
We specialize in creating tools that solve the hardest problems in high-density online environments—handling high concurrency, automated safety, real-time message pipelines, and community management at massive scale.
